Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Namna ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways when you use the Service:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or leave a comment
  • Automatically collected information — such as your IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, gathered through cookies and similar technologies
  • Third-party information — such as analytics data provided by services we use to understand how the site is used

1.1 Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, understand site traffic, and improve the Service. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver our newsletter and respond to inquiries
  • Personalize and improve the content and features we offer
  • Analyze site traffic and understand reader interests
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Service — such as email delivery or analytics providers — under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law.

4. Your Choices

4.1 Newsletter

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link included in every email we send.

4.2 Access & Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us via our Contact page.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page. See also our Terms of Service.
