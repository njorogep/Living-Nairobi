Inside the Season's Most Talked-About Gala

The guests, the looks and the moments everyone is still talking about.

By Namna Staff · June 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Some nights are simply a party. Others become the night everyone refers to for the rest of the year. The annual Midnight Garden Gala was the second kind.

The arrival

Guests arrived at a candlelit garden in Karen from seven o'clock, walking a path lined with lanterns and white orchids. The dress code said black tie; most people took it as a challenge.

The looks

Gold was the colour of the night, from sequinned gowns to embroidered kanzus. Several Nairobi designers dressed guests for the evening, and the local labels drew more compliments than the imported ones.

The dinner

Four courses were served at one long table under the trees, with a menu that moved from coastal seafood to highland lamb. Between courses a string quartet played covers of Kenyan pop songs, which got the biggest cheers of the night.

The moment

At midnight the lights dropped, the confetti fell and the band took over. By one in the morning the dance floor was full and the shoes were off.

“We wanted people to leave feeling like they had been part of something, not just attended something.” — Event host

The organisers have already confirmed next year's date. If you want an invitation, start being interesting now.
