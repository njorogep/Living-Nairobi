Quiet Luxury

The well-made pieces that anchor a wardrobe built to last, and where to find them in Nairobi.

By Namna Staff · June 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Trends come and go at the speed of a scroll. Quiet luxury is the opposite idea: buy fewer things, buy them well, and wear them for years. No logos, no noise, just good fabric and good fit.

1. The perfect white T-shirt

Heavyweight cotton, a collar that holds its shape and a length that works tucked or untucked. It sounds simple, and it is the hardest piece to get right.

2. A tailored blazer

One well-cut blazer in navy or camel does more work than five trend pieces. Wear it over a T-shirt on Friday and over a shirt on Monday. A good tailor in town can make an off-the-rack jacket look made to measure.

3. Straight-leg trousers

Not too slim, not too wide. In linen for the hot months and wool blends for the cool Nairobi evenings.

4. Leather shoes that age well

Loafers or clean sneakers in real leather. Look after them and they will look better in five years than they do today.

5. One statement accessory

A watch, a bag or a piece of jewellery made by a local craftsperson. It is the one thing people will ask you about.

“Style is what you keep wearing after the trend has moved on.” — Wanjiku Mbugua, stylist

Start with one piece, wear it often, and build from there. A wardrobe built slowly is a wardrobe that lasts.
