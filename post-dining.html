Where to Dine Right Now

The openings and old favourites setting the pace on Nairobi's dining scene this season.

By Namna Staff · June 13, 2026 · 6 min read

Nairobi eats out more adventurously every year. New kitchens open almost weekly, chefs who trained abroad are coming home, and the old favourites are raising their game to keep up. We spent a month eating our way across the city to find the tables worth booking right now.

1. Moto Kitchen, Westlands

Everything at Moto passes over an open flame, from charred sukuma with smoked butter to whole tilapia wrapped in banana leaf. Sit at the counter and watch the kitchen work; it is the best show in town.

2. Saa Nane, Kilimani

A twelve-seat chef's table that serves one tasting menu a night. The menu changes with the market, and the chef talks you through every course. Book two weeks ahead.

3. The Verandah, Karen

Long lunches under jacaranda trees, a wine list that leans South African, and a lemon tart people drive across town for. Come hungry and do not make afternoon plans.

4. Kaa Chini, CBD

A no-frills lunch spot with a queue out the door by half past twelve. The pilau is the reason; the fresh juice is the bonus.

5 to 8: the ones to watch

A rooftop grill in Upper Hill, a plant-forward bistro in Lavington, a Swahili coast kitchen in Gigiri and a late-night ramen bar in Westlands round out our list. Each opened in the past six months and each is already hard to get into.

“Nairobi diners want a story on the plate. Where the food came from matters as much as how it tastes.” — Chef Amina Otieno

Whatever you are in the mood for, the city has a table for it. The trick is booking before everyone else does.
