About Us

Namna is a digital-first lifestyle and culture media platform for a curious, culturally aware and socially engaged generation.

We explore People · Dining · Style · Travel · Events · The View through the lens of a country that is constantly evolving.

Because lifestyle is more than what we buy.

It is how we spend our time, where we go, what we eat, what we wear, the people we meet, the places we discover and the things we choose to care about.

Namna exists to make sense of that world.

Our Mission

We create thoughtful, useful and beautifully presented stories that help our readers navigate contemporary life.

From the people shaping the culture around us to the restaurants worth lingering in, the clothes worth knowing, the places worth going and the conversations worth having — Namna is interested in the way we live now.

Our aim is simple: to give readers stories that are worth their time, attention and curiosity.

What We Cover

People

The people behind the titles, businesses, ideas and influence shaping contemporary Kenya.

The View

Ideas, observations, conversations and stories about the things changing how we live.
