About Us

Namna is a digital lifestyle and editorial magazine dedicated to the people, places and ideas shaping life in Kenya's capital. We cover fashion, beauty, travel, dining, culture and wellness through the lens of a city that moves fast and dresses well.

Founded on the belief that Nairobi's creative energy deserves the same editorial care as any major global city, we bring together writers, photographers and stylists who live and work here — reporting on the restaurants worth booking, the designers worth watching, and the weekend escapes worth planning.

Our Mission

We exist to help Nairobians live better, more considered lives. Every story is reported with the same question in mind: does this genuinely help our reader spend their time, money and attention well? From a five-minute read on quiet luxury to a deep dive on the city's art scene, our goal is always the same — thoughtful, useful, beautifully presented content.

What We Cover

  • Fashion & personal style, from runway reports to wardrobe essentials
  • Beauty & wellness routines suited to Nairobi's climate and pace
  • Travel guides, weekend escapes and city breaks
  • Dining reviews and the restaurants, bars and rooftops worth your evening
  • Arts, culture and the creative community driving the city forward

Our Team

Namna is produced by a small, dedicated team of local editors, contributing writers and photographers. We work closely with the city's designers, chefs, curators and entrepreneurs to bring authentic, first-hand stories to our readers — never syndicated, always local.

Get in Touch

Have a story idea, a correction, or a business you think we should feature? We'd love to hear from you. Reach our editorial team any time — details are on our Contact page — and don't forget to subscribe to the newsletter so you never miss a story.
